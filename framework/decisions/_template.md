## Context

What forced a choice. The constraint, the ambiguity, or the thing that was
underspecified. If a reader would ask "why is this even a question?", answer that
here.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated so a reader can act on it without reading the code.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what a future change would cost.
Include the unpleasant consequences — a record that only lists upsides is
marketing, not a decision log.

## Alternatives rejected

The options that were genuinely considered, and why each lost. "We didn't think
of anything else" is a valid entry and more honest than inventing alternatives.

## Notes

Anything a future engineer would otherwise have to rediscover by experiment.
